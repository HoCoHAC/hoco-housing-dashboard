Write an evidence JSON file (e.g. /home/user/workspace/hcar_evidence.json) with keys: source="HCAR / Bright MLS", metric="median_sold_price", geography="Howard County, Maryland", period="YYYY-MM", price=<numeric>, verification_price=<numeric>, verification_period="YYYY-MM", report_url=<exact HTTPS hcar.org Detailed Report URL>, verification_url=<different exact HTTPS hcar.org Insight Report URL>. Retain dated evidence in the repository. Never fabricate a second observation or reuse the prior month's report as verification.

STEP 2 — RUN THE CHECKER (IT MAKES THE DECISION, NOT YOU)
cd /home/user/workspace && python3 rate_check.py --price <VERIFIED_MEDIAN_SOLD_PRICE> --hcar-evidence /home/user/workspace/hcar_evidence.json
Pass the price as a plain number, e.g. --price 639000. If HCAR cannot be verified or the reports disagree, do not supply either price argument: run a rate-only check on the already-HCAR dashboard, preserve the displayed sold price and its source month, and explain the limitation. Never fall back to Realtor.com for the headline.

The script reads the specific county-median-sold-price KPI and displayed rate from index.html, fetches the latest Freddie Mac PMMS 30-year fixed rate, applies the quarter-point threshold to the rate, and applies no threshold to the verified sold price. It validates the HCAR evidence's exact values, periods and source URLs, appends the reading with source metadata to rate_history.json, and prints a DECISION. It must not choose the most frequent dollar value across the HTML or compare sold prices against a listing-price research file.

Exit codes:
  0  HOLD    — nothing to change. Skip steps 3, 4, 5 and 6, but STILL RUN step 2.5. Then go to step 7.
  10 UPDATE  — apply the figures it printed.
  3  BLOCKED — the HCAR evidence is missing, inconsistent, older than displayed, future-dated, or otherwise invalid. DO NOT EDIT ANYTHING. Correct the evidence from the actual reports before a changed-input retry. There is no bypass flag. If unresolved, change nothing and report it.
  1  ERROR   — the check itself failed. Report the error, change nothing.

Do not second-guess the decision, recompute the gap, or do any of this arithmetic by hand. The threshold is measured against the DISPLAYED rate, not the previous reading, so small monthly moves accumulate and fire on their own. When the script says UPDATE it prints every recalculated figure — P&I, property tax, PMI, total monthly, income required, down payment, cash to close — using the page's own formulas and rounding. Use those printed numbers verbatim. It also handles the case where the price moved but the rate did not: it recalculates at the OLD displayed rate and says so. Never substitute a rate that did not cross the threshold.

STEP 2.5 — CHECK FOR NEWER EDITIONS OF THE STATIC DATA (ALWAYS RUN, READ ONLY)
This step NEVER edits the page. It only looks and reports. Each item below records what the dashboard currently displays; check whether a newer edition has appeared and report only the differences. Verified all-current on September 2, 2026.

- ACS 5-year (B25003 tenure, B25070 and B25091 cost burden, S1811 disability earnings). Displayed: 2020-2024, released January 29, 2026. Check https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/news/data-releases.html for a 2021-2025 edition.
- ACS 1-year (28,952 residents with a disability, 8.7%, 24.2%). Displayed: 2024 1-year. As of September 2026 the 2025 1-year release date was still undetermined while Census worked through a new Commerce disclosure-avoidance order, so this one may be delayed or may not appear at all. Check https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/news/updates.html
- HUD CHAS (cost burden by race and ethnicity). Displayed: 2018-2022, which HUD re-released on December 23, 2025 and which is still the newest edition. Check https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/cp.html for a 2019-2023 or 2020-2024 edition. IMPORTANT: if one appears, TWO published statements that HUD has not yet released a CHAS edition based on 2020-2024 data become factually wrong and need revising -- the 'Race and ethnicity' note in the Methodology card, and the 'Note on vintage' entry under 'Housing Cost Burdens by Race & Ethnicity' in the Full Source List card. Flag both explicitly.
- SSI federal benefit rate ($994/mo, $11,928/yr). SSA announces the next COLA on October 14, 2026, and the resulting rate takes effect in January 2027. Check https://www.ssa.gov/oact/cola/SSI.html
- HCHC payment standards (2BR range $1,947-$3,069). New standards normally take effect January 1. Check https://www.howardcountyhousing.org
- MIHU annual analysis. Displayed: the 2025 edition, published April 2026. Check https://www.howardcountymd.gov/housing-community-development/publications-reports for a 2026 edition.
- Howard County Rental Survey (every rent, utility allowance, income-required and AMI figure, plus the sector wage chart). Displayed: the 2024 survey, built on November 2023 field data. It has no fixed publication schedule. Check the same county publications page.
- Homelessness / CoC MD-504. Two separate HUD releases feed this card and they arrive at different times of year. (a) Point-in-time counts. Displayed: the 2025 count, 147 total / 96 sheltered / 51 unsheltered, taken January 28, 2025. DO NOT ASSUME A DECEMBER RELEASE AND DO NOT TREAT THIS AS A SEASONAL CHECK. HUD published the 2022, 2023 and 2024 counts each December, then broke that pattern badly: the 2025 count did not appear until May 29, 2026, about sixteen months after its January 2025 count night. So the 2026 count could land anywhere from December 2026 to well into 2027, and this item must be checked every single month. The cheapest test is the CoC-level workbook filename, which carries the latest year in it: https://www.huduser.gov/portal/sites/default/files/xls/2007-2025-PIT-Counts-by-CoC.xlsb is what feeds the chart today, so a 2007-2026 filename appearing is the tell. Also check https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/ahar.html, bearing in mind that page lagged the actual 2025 release by months and still listed 2024 as current well afterwards -- so a stale AHAR page is NOT evidence that no new count exists. Confirm against the MD-504 PopSub report for the new year, https://files.hudexchange.info/reports/published/CoC_PopSub_CoC_MD-504-2026_MD_2026.pdf; if that returns a PDF, the count is out. IF A NEW COUNT IS OUT, GO TO STEP 2.6 -- you are authorised to apply it. (b) System performance measures. Displayed: federal FY2024, average length of time homeless 162.8 days rounded to 163, median 101 days. Check https://www.hudexchange.info/programs/coc/system-performance-measures/ and the FY2025 profile at https://files.hudexchange.info/reports/published/CoC_Perf_CoC_MD-504-2025_MD_2025.pdf -- if that URL returns a PDF rather than a 403 or 404, FY2025 has been published. Note that huduser.gov and files.hudexchange.info both need the browser User-Agent header. There is also a county source with no fixed schedule: The Path Toward Zero 2025-2030 supplies the entries and exits, the 960 active participants and the 59% / 21% racial comparison, and the County may publish annual progress reporting against it. Check https://www.howardcountymd.gov/homeless-services/coalition-end-homelessness-continuum-care

Do not fetch these one at a time in a long serial chain if you can batch them. Do not spend more than a few minutes here. If a source is unreachable, say so in the notification and move on rather than retrying repeatedly.

STEP 2.6 — APPLY A NEW POINT-IN-TIME COUNT (only when STEP 2.5 confirmed one)
This is the ONE static figure the user has authorised this task to update on its own. It runs independently of the rate checker: it can fire on a HOLD (exit 0), and if it does, that counts as 'a figure changed' for the purposes of steps 4, 5 and 6.

First, get the numbers from HUD, not from a summary or a news story. Read the MD-504 PopSub report for the new year and take four values: total sheltered, total unsheltered, the overall total, and the count date. Cross-check the same three counts against the CoC-level workbook for the new year. The two must agree exactly. If they disagree, or if either source cannot be read, CHANGE NOTHING and report the discrepancy in the notification -- a mismatch means one of the two is a draft.

Sanity gates before you edit. Any of these failing means stop and report instead:
- sheltered + unsheltered must equal the published total.
- The total should be within roughly 60-260. MD-504's eleven-year range is 82 to 214, so anything far outside that is more likely a misread of the wrong CoC or the wrong row than a real result.
- The count date must be a January date in the new year. If it is not, you are probably looking at a prior year's report.
- The year must be exactly one more than the last entry in the PIT array. Do not skip a year, and do not overwrite an existing entry.

Then make these edits and only these. The strings below are what is on the page today; a later run should expect the year numbers to have moved on.
- The PIT array in the JavaScript: append one entry in the existing style, e.g. { yr: '2026', sh: <sheltered>, un: <unsheltered> }. Append only. Do not recompute or 'correct' any earlier entry. If HUD suppressed the unsheltered count again -- an em dash in the PopSub unsheltered cells -- set un to null, not 0, exactly as 2021 does, and add the same asterisk-and-hatch treatment for that year by adding it to the non-comparable handling rather than replacing 2021's.
- First KPI tile: the label '2025 Point-in-Time Count' takes the new year; the value 147 takes the new total; the sub 'People counted on the night of January 28, 2025 &mdash; up from 130 a year earlier' takes the new count date and compares against 147, with 'up from' or 'down from' whichever is now true.
- Second KPI tile: the value 51 takes the new unsheltered figure, and the sub '35% of the count, down from 62 in 2024' takes the recomputed share and compares against 51. Compute the share as unsheltered divided by total, rounded to the nearest whole percent, and state that arithmetic in the notification so it can be checked.
- The chart subcap 'Point-in-time count by shelter status, 2015&ndash;2025' takes the new end year.
- The aria-label on the chartHomelessPIT canvas: extend the year-by-year total list with the new year and update the closing sentences about the unsheltered trend so the described figures match the chart. A screen-reader description that still says 2025 is the newest year is a real defect, not a cosmetic one.
- In the Full Source List card, under the 'Homelessness: The Path Home' heading: update the workbook filename and URL to the new edition, add the new year's PopSub link alongside the existing ones, refresh the retrieval date, and revise the final 'Note on vintage' entry, which currently asserts that 2025 is the most recent count HUD has published for MD-504. Leave the system-performance-measures entry alone unless STEP 2.5 separately confirmed a new one.
- Advance the 'Data as of' badge and the matching footer date, since a figure genuinely changed.

Do NOT touch the average-time-homeless tile, the entries-over-exits tile, the FLOW array, either callout, or the Methodology note's 295-person figure. Those are different HUD and county releases and a new PIT count says nothing about them.
